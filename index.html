Seeing instruction clearly

These tools give you and your team a common lens through which to understand and imagine your instruction.

The 6 LeapsOne-pager
The 6 Leaps

The six dimensions of the student experience that the science of learning and young people themselves tell us drive deep engagement: whole-child focus, connection and community, high expectations with rigorous learning, relevance, customization, and agency.

When to use itLeverage this to reflect on which Leaps you and others are seeing at Gulliver, where and how, and which Leaps you'd like to see more of.
SOLD PrimerSlide deck
Science of Learning & Development Primer

This primer provides an overview of the research behind our Insight Cards: cognition, motivation, identity, and individual variability, and how they interact. Includes a short video and a school example from Van Ness Elementary in DC.

When to use it30 minutes of pre-work before your team engages with the Insight Cards, so the observations sit on shared footing.
Insight CardsSlide deck
Learning Science Insight Cards

18 cards. Each holds a research principles about how people learn, and questions for design. Five physical decks are on their way to you and your team so you can use the real cards.

When to use itBefore and during classroom visits, alone or with your instructional coaches. Pick 3 or 4 cards, visit classrooms, and sort which principles are most and least honored and in what ways.

Learning alongside your team

Right now you're on a discovery journey. The tools below invite your team to sit alongside you in shared learning experiences, supporting stronger alignment as you look at the same things together.

Shadow a StudentGuide
Shadow a Student

Live one school day as a Gulliver student lives it: the schedule, the transitions, the cognitive demand, the bright spots, the challenges. This offers a structure protocol with reflection prompts for a debrief.

When to use itOnce yourself, early. Then send each member of your leadership team. Debrief together within the week.
InterviewsTemplates + question bank
Interview Templates + Question Bank

Ready-made questions and a template for one-on-one, 45-minute conversations with students, plus banks for alumni, staff, caregivers, and community members.

When to use itBuild a custom guide from the student bank, then aim for 4 or 5 students across divisions in your first months. Each leadership team member does at least one.
